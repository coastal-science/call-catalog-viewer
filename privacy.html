Privacy

This page describes how the Orca Sound Call Catalogue website (https://orca.research.sfu.ca/call-library) handles information when you visit or contact us. The site is part of SFU’s HALLO / Coastal Science research work and does not require an account to use the catalogue.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-4ZT5PMCN8Z) to understand how the catalogue is used—for example which pages are visited and roughly how often. Google Analytics typically collects information such as:

  • pages viewed and approximate time on site
  • general device and browser information
  • approximate geographic region (not a precise address)
  • referring site or search terms, when available

This may involve cookies or similar technologies set by Google. We use the data to improve the site and support the research programme; we do not sell it. Google’s processing is also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

Cookies

Aside from any cookies used by Google Analytics (and standard technical cookies your browser may use for normal browsing), this site does not use advertising cookies or build a personal marketing profile. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; doing so may affect analytics but should not prevent you from browsing the catalogue content.

If you prefer not to be included in Google Analytics across the web, Google provides a browser opt-out add-on.

Contact form

If you send a message through our contact form, the information you enter (such as name, email address, and message) is delivered to the catalogue team so we can respond. That submission is handled by the form service we use (FormSubmit) and then by our email. Please do not include sensitive personal information you would not want sent by email.

Technical problems with the website can instead be reported via GitHub Issues, which is subject to GitHub’s own terms and privacy policy.

Catalogue content

Audio, spectrograms, and related catalogue materials are research content made available under the site’s licence. Using the catalogue does not require you to provide personal details beyond what is described above.

Questions

If you have questions about this privacy notice, please contact us.
